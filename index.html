The opportunity

The first responder wins the deal.

When a company raises a round, launches a product, or hires a new CMO, that's the moment they need PR — and the window is short. Kickstand is not currently systematically catching those moments across every vertical, every day. Signal Scout does, and hands each sales lead a ready-to-send message before a competitor even notices.

Every card carries what the lead needs to act in 30 seconds

  1. The signal, with a link to the source.
  2. The company and why it scored as a fit.
  3. The best contact — name, title, LinkedIn, and email.
  4. A message in the lead's exact voice, referencing the real signal and nothing invented.
  5. One button that sends and logs the touch to HubSpot.

Messages are always suggested, never auto-sent — a human stays in the loop.

Built to ship

Editable by the team. Cheap to run.

Lives in GitHub, not in one person's account

Anyone at Kickstand can open config/verticals.yaml and config/voice_profiles.yaml to change recipients, keywords, or a lead's tone — no coding. GitHub Actions runs it on schedule for free.

Under budget by design

$0 to demo for the submission. ~$50–90/mo live — Claude for the writing (~$10–25), a contact finder, plus HubSpot, email, and scheduling that are all free. Comfortably inside the $200/mo ceiling and the $10K implementation budget — and a fraction of the alternatives above.

Contact source options: Hunter.io API ~$34/mo (recommended), or upgrade Apollo to its Organization tier ~$357/mo (3-seat minimum) — only worth it if Kickstand expands the whole sales stack.
